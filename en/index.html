Company formation options

Choose the right company formation model.

We offer two primary formation options. The most suitable arrangement will depend on the client’s objectives, document readiness and the company’s intended activities.

01

Company registered directly for the client

The Australian company is registered from the outset using the client’s details. Once the required information has been provided, the client is appointed as director and becomes the shareholder of the company.

KiwiAus coordinates the registration process, prepares the company documentation and assists the client through the principal formation steps.

Suitable for clients who are ready to provide the information and documents required for registration and applicable checks.
02

Pre-established Australian business structure

The company is initially incorporated and prepared by our Australian team. Agreed company records and corporate documentation are organised before the structure is transferred to its new owner.

Once the client’s information has been received, applicable checks have been completed and the agreed procedures are finalised, the director and shareholder changes are formally recorded.

Suitable for clients who require an Australian company with its initial formation completed, documentation prepared and a structured transfer process in place.
